# Prototype

`sea.html` is the first **throwaway prototype**: one file, no build step, made
to discover what the product should feel like. Open it in a browser to play.

It is a reference, not the product. The real site will be planned and built
step by step; nothing here is reused without being reviewed.

## What we learned from it

**Keep:**

- The interactive sea, the headline, and how the name changes with what you type
- Turning elements on and off and interacting with them
- Sound (not yet tested properly)

**Drop:**

- The time slider, moon and tide readouts — the sky should just follow the
  visitor's clock, with a greeting instead of numbers
- Unknown words floating as letters

**Not decided yet:** realistic 3D ocean, fluid effects, a scrolling dive minigame.

**Added after:** phone tilt, a Scribblenauts-style word list with interactions
between things, and a log of submitted words to decide what to add next.
