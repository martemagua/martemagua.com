# Vision

> **martemagua** is an interactive sea on a web page, for me, my friends and
> family. Every word you write becomes something the sea has: *o mar tem ___*.
> There's nothing to sign up for, no score and no ads. You touch it, tilt it and
> type into it, and the sea answers. The more words you discover, the more the
> things in it start playing with each other and with their surroundings.
>
> It is also my learning project. It's built together with AI, but understood by
> me from end to end, so it stays clean, easy to explain, and easy to grow.

## Who it is for

- **Me, my friends and family.** Fun for a few minutes, worth coming back to
  every so often.
- **Me as a learner.** I want to know the technology and logic behind every
  part and be able to explain exactly what is going on.

## Principles

These settle arguments later. When two options compete, the one that fits
these better wins.

1. **Phone first, works everywhere.** Touch and tilt are the main controls. A
   mouse and keyboard are a fallback.
2. **Alive before you touch it.** The page moves and invites you in on its own.
3. **Every word gets a response**, even ones the sea doesn't know yet.
4. **Innocent and private.** No accounts, no tracking, no personal data.
   Progress is remembered only on the visitor's own device.
5. **Easy to explain beats clever.** If I can't explain how something works, it
   doesn't ship.
6. **Brazilian Portuguese first.** English and German come later, and only where
   the jokes survive translation.

## What it is not

- Not a portal to my homelab.
- Not a social network.
- Not an advertising page.
- Not a competition — no leaderboard.

## Success looks like

- **For people:** friends and family come back without being reminded, and
  their unknown words keep turning into new things to add.
- **For the code:** I can explain every part of it. Adding a new word is one
  small, obvious change.
- **For quality:** it runs smoothly on an ordinary phone and loads fast on
  mobile data.

## Constraints

- **Hosting:** my own server. Budget is minimal; paid services are welcome for
  review but are not the default.
- **Devices:** phones first; must still work on tablets and desktops.
- **Language:** Brazilian Portuguese at launch (accepting European Portuguese
  spellings and unaccented typing). English and German later.
- **Tools:** Claude (subscription + tokens) as the development partner.
